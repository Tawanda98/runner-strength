          How to progress

          • Weeks 1–2: learn the moves, 2 rounds is fine.
          • Weeks 3–4: full 3 rounds.
          • Week 5+: slow each rep down (3 s down, 1 s up), or wear a backpack with books or water bottles.
          • Once 15 reps feels easy, make the move harder. Don't just keep adding reps.
